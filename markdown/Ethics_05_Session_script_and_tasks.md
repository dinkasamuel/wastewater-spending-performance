# Session script, tasks and questionnaire

**Duration:** about 30 minutes. **Recording:** none. Notes are taken by the researcher.
**Participant code:** P__  **Date:** ______  **Mode:** in person / Teams  **Dashboard version:** v__

The task IDs link each task to the acceptance tests in the requirements register (Appendix B).

## A. Opening (2 minutes)

> "Thank you for taking part. I'm testing a dashboard I built, not you, so if something is confusing, that's useful for me to know. Please think aloud as you work: tell me what you're looking at and what you expect to happen. You can skip anything or stop at any time. Nothing is recorded; I'll just take notes under a code, not your name. Do you have any questions before we start?"

Confirm that the consent form has been completed. If it has not, stop here.

## B. Needs questions (5 minutes, before seeing the dashboard)

N1. If you wanted to know whether a water company is looking after its sewers well, what would you want to see?

N2. What would make you trust, or distrust, a chart comparing companies?

N3. How would you like uncertainty, meaning "we're not sure", to be shown to you?

## D. Questionnaire (8 minutes)

Ask the participant to rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).

**Scoring:**
- For odd-numbered items, the score is the rating minus 1.
- For even-numbered items, the score is 5 minus the rating.
- Add the ten scores and multiply the total by 2.5, giving a score from 0 to 100.

### D2. Interpretation check

Q1. True or false: "Companies that spent more caused fewer sewer floods." *Correct: false. The dashboard shows associations, not causes.*

Q2. A result's 95% range runs from −0.5 to +0.8. Does this show a clear link? *Correct: no. The range includes zero, so the direction is uncertain.*

Q3. Is "spending per connected property" adjusted for inflation? *Correct: yes, it is at 2017-18 prices.*

### D3. Open questions

O1. What was the most confusing part of the dashboard?

O2. What one change would make it more useful to you?

## E. Debrief (1 minute)

> "Thank you. To repeat: the dashboard shows associations in public data, not proof that spending causes changes. If you'd like to withdraw your answers, email me within 7 days. Would you like a summary of the findings?"
