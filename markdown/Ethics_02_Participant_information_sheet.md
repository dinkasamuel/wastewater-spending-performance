# Participant information sheet

*Paste into UWE's "Student research participant information sheet" template, which carries the UWE logo.*

**Study title:** Evaluating a dashboard about water companies' wastewater spending and performance

You are invited to take part in a short study for my MSc Data Science dissertation at UWE Bristol. Please read this sheet before deciding. You can ask me any questions.

## What is the study about?

I have built an interactive dashboard in Microsoft Power BI. It shows how England's nine water and sewerage companies spent money on their sewer networks, and how often problems such as sewer flooding and sewer collapses occurred. The data are public statistics published by the regulator, Ofwat.

I want to find out whether people who are not water-industry experts can understand the dashboard and read its results correctly. **I am testing the dashboard, not you.** There are no right or wrong ways to use it.

## Why have I been invited?

You are an adult who is comfortable reading charts and tables but does not work in water-industry regulation. That makes you one of the dashboard's intended users. I am inviting 3–5 people.

## Do I have to take part?

No. Taking part is entirely voluntary. Saying no will not affect you in any way. If you agree, you can stop at any time during the session without giving a reason.

## What will happen if I take part?

- **One session of about 30 minutes,** in person at [LOCATION] or on Microsoft Teams, at a time that suits you.
- **Three short questions** about what you would want to learn from a dashboard like this.
- **Five tasks** using the dashboard, such as finding a value or interpreting a chart, while saying aloud what you are thinking.
- **A short questionnaire:** 10 standard usability statements, three questions about what the results mean, and two open questions.

**The session will not be recorded:** no audio, video or screen recording. I will write notes, labelled with a code such as "P2" rather than your name.

## Are there any risks or benefits?

There are no expected risks beyond everyday ones. Some tasks may feel tricky; that tells me the dashboard needs improving. You can skip any task or question.

There is no payment. You may find it interesting to see how public data on water companies can be explored.

## What happens to my information?

- **Your name, email and signature** are used only to arrange the session and record your consent. They are kept separately from your answers, in a secure UWE OneDrive folder that only I can access.
- **Your answers** are anonymous, identified only by a code. They may be summarised or briefly quoted in my dissertation, without anything that could identify you.
- **Only my supervisor and I** will see the raw notes.
- **More detail** is in the privacy notice: [attach UWE student research privacy notice].

## Can I withdraw?

Yes. You can withdraw during the session, or **within 7 days after it**, by emailing me. You do not need to give a reason, and your notes will be deleted. After 7 days your anonymous answers will already be combined with others, so they cannot be removed.

## What happens to the results?

The results will be used to improve the dashboard and will be reported in my dissertation, submitted to UWE Bristol in January 2027. If you would like a short summary of the findings, tell me.
