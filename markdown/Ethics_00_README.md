## The UWE route for this study (checked on uwe.ac.uk, 18 Nov 2026)

1. For taught Masters projects, the **supervisor** completes UWE's *Student Ethical Review Record* (SharePoint, UWE login) and decides whether the project is low or high risk.
2. **Low risk** means the supervisor approves it. **High risk** means the supervisor submits a full application through Worktribe, which UWE says takes **4 weeks, or up to 6 over Christmas**. That would rule out the study before the deadline.
3. The study below is designed to be **low risk**:
   - adult volunteers who are not vulnerable;
   - no sensitive topics;
   - no deception;
   - no audio or video recording;
   - anonymous notes only;
   - no incentives;
   - about 30 minutes.
4. UWE requires its **own templates**, including the UWE logo, for the participant information sheet, consent form and privacy notice. The files here contain the **content** to paste into those templates, found on UWE's "Research ethics policies, procedures and guidance" page under *For undergraduate and postgraduate taught (Masters) students*:
   - Consent forms (DOC)
   - Student research participant information sheets (DOC)
   - Guidance for students on drafting a research privacy notice (PDF)
   - Data Protection guidance for UWE Bristol students (PDF)
